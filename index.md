# Personal Marketplace Research Tool — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 28, 2026**

## Overview

Personal Marketplace Research Tool is a privately operated application developed for individual use. It runs locally and is not publicly accessible or distributed to other users.

## Information Accessed

The application uses eBay's official APIs to retrieve publicly available marketplace information, including item listings, descriptions, prices, images, and related details.

The application does not require access to personal eBay accounts, purchase histories, or payment information.

## Data Storage

Application preferences, saved searches, and relevant marketplace information may be stored locally on the user's device. Information retrieved through eBay's APIs is handled according to eBay's applicable data usage and retention requirements.

## Information Sharing

The application does not sell, distribute, or publicly share personal information. Search requests are transmitted to eBay as necessary to retrieve marketplace information.

## Security

The application operates locally and is not exposed as a publicly accessible service. Application credentials and locally stored information are not intentionally made publicly available.

## Data Retention

Locally maintained application settings and preferences may be retained until deleted. Marketplace data is retained only as permitted by applicable API terms.
